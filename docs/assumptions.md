# Assumptions and data-quality notes

| Assumption | Baseline | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Material input | 12 kg | Includes machining loss above the 10 kg finished component |
| Primary aluminium share | 90% | Dominates the baseline climate result |
| Manufacturing electricity | 18 kWh | Represents simplified machining/production energy |
| Supplier transport | 800 km | Simplified road-freight leg |
| EoL recovery rate | 85% | Reported as circularity flow only; no avoided-burden credit |

The Ecodesign scenarios deliberately change one or more design variables so the effect of lightweighting, recycled content, logistics, and recovery can be compared.

These are portfolio assumptions, not data from The Exploration Company or any aerospace supplier.
