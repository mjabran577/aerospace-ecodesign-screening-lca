# Executive Summary — Aerospace Ecodesign Screening LCA

## Objective
Compare a baseline generic aluminium aerospace enclosure with three Ecodesign alternatives.

## Results
- Baseline screening result: **170.6**
- High recycled content: **65.6** (61.5% lower)
- Lightweight design: **144.9** (15.1% lower)
- Combined Ecodesign: **55.4** (67.5% lower)

## Interpretation
Primary aluminium is the dominant baseline hotspot. The combined design performs best in this screening model because it reduces material input, raises recycled content, lowers manufacturing electricity, shortens road freight, and improves end-of-life recovery.

## Limitations
This is a portfolio screening model. Public factors have different boundaries, the model focuses mainly on climate change, and no avoided-burden recycling credit is used. It should not be used for external environmental claims.
